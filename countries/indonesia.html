Overview

The Dutch East Indies — modern Indonesia — was occupied by Japan from March 1942, ending centuries of Dutch colonial rule. Allied POWs were interned under brutal conditions in camps across Java and Sumatra, and the occupation reshaped the post-war order, fuelling Indonesian independence after Japan's surrender.
