WWII Film Locations in France

39 locations · 24 titles

Overview

France fell to Germany in just six weeks in May-June 1940, leading to four years of occupation, collaboration, and resistance. The country was liberated between June and September 1944 following the D-Day landings in Normandy and Operation Dragoon in the south — battles that define more WWII cinema than almost any other theatre.

CinemaMapped has mapped 39 real WWII film locations in France, drawn from 24 titles. Each pin marks the actual historical place depicted in the film - not a filming location, but where the events happened.
