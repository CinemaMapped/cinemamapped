Overview

Tunisia became the final battleground of the North African campaign in late 1942 and early 1943, as Allied forces caught the Axis between the Anglo-American force from the west and Montgomery's Eighth Army from the east. The Tunisian campaign ended in May 1943 with the surrender of 250,000 Axis troops — a defeat comparable to Stalingrad.
