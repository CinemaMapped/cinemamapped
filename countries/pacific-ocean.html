Overview

The Pacific Ocean was the defining theatre of the war between the United States and Japan, fought across thousands of miles of open water and island chains from Hawaii to the Philippine Sea. The battles of Midway, Leyte Gulf, and the island-hopping campaign towards Japan itself determined the outcome of the Pacific War.
