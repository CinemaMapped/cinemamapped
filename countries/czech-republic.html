WWII Film Locations in Czech Republic

7 locations · 5 titles

Overview

Czechoslovakia was the first country dismembered by Hitler's expansionism — first the Sudetenland in 1938, then the remaining Czech lands as the Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia in 1939. Prague was the base of Reinhard Heydrich, the Reich Protector assassinated by Czech and Slovak paratroopers in Operation Anthropoid in 1942.

CinemaMapped has mapped 7 real WWII film locations in Czech Republic, drawn from 5 titles. Each pin marks the actual historical place depicted in the film - not a filming location, but where the events happened.
