Overview

Spain remained officially neutral but was ideologically aligned with the Axis, sending the Blue Division of volunteers to fight on the Eastern Front alongside Germany. Spain also served as a vital escape route for Allied POWs, downed airmen, and Jewish refugees fleeing through occupied France via the Pyrenees.
