WWII Film Locations in Poland

45 locations · 23 titles

Overview

Poland was the first country invaded by Germany in September 1939 and among the last to be liberated in 1945. It suffered the highest proportional death toll of any nation in the war — around 17 percent of its pre-war population — and was home to the majority of the Nazi extermination camps, making it the epicentre of the Holocaust.

CinemaMapped has mapped 45 real WWII film locations in Poland, drawn from 23 titles. Each pin marks the actual historical place depicted in the film - not a filming location, but where the events happened.
