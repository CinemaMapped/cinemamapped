Overview

Finland fought two wars against the Soviet Union during WWII: the Winter War of 1939-40, in which Finnish forces inflicted devastating casualties on the Red Army before being forced to cede territory, and the Continuation War of 1941-44, fought alongside Germany to retake the lost land. Finland's resistance defined its national identity.
