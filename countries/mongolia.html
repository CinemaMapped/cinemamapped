Overview

Mongolia, then a Soviet satellite state, saw some of the early war's most significant engagements in the form of the Battles of Khalkhin Gol against Japan in 1939 — a decisive Soviet victory that shaped Japan's decision not to attack the USSR from the east in 1941, arguably changing the outcome of the whole war.
