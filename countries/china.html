WWII Film Locations in China

Overview

China had been at war with Japan since 1937, suffering invasions, occupations, and atrocities including the Nanjing Massacre. The Second Sino-Japanese War merged into WWII after Pearl Harbor, making China a key Allied theatre in which millions died and which shaped the post-war order across East Asia.
