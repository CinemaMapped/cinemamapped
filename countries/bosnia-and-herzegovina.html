Overview

During the German and Italian occupation of Yugoslavia, Bosnia was part of the puppet Independent State of Croatia, which implemented its own genocidal policies against Serbs, Jews, and Roma. The Yugoslav Partisans under Tito fought some of their most decisive engagements here, including the Battle of the Neretva in 1943.
