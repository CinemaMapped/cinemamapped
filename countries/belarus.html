Overview

Belarus was at the heart of some of the most savage fighting and war crimes of the Eastern Front. German forces killed approximately 25 percent of the pre-war population through combat, starvation, reprisal massacres, and the Holocaust. Come and See, set in Belarus, is widely considered the greatest anti-war film ever made.
