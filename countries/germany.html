WWII Film Locations in Germany

95 locations · 45 titles

Overview

Germany launched the Second World War on September 1, 1939, and by 1942 its forces occupied most of continental Europe. The Third Reich collapsed in April-May 1945 under Soviet assault from the east and Allied pressure from the west, ending with Hitler's suicide in the Berlin bunker and unconditional surrender on May 8.

CinemaMapped has mapped 95 real WWII film locations in Germany, drawn from 45 titles. Each pin marks the actual historical place depicted in the film - not a filming location, but where the events happened.
