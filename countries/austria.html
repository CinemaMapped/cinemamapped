Overview

Austria was absorbed into the Third Reich by the Anschluss in March 1938, its population largely welcoming the union. It was a significant provider of manpower and resources to the German war machine. After liberation in 1945, Austria successfully cultivated the myth of being Hitler's first victim rather than a willing participant — a narrative that shaped its politics for decades.
