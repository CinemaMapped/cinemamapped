Overview

The United States entered the war after Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941 and became the decisive industrial and military power of the Allied coalition. From the Pacific island-hopping campaign to D-Day to the skies over Germany, American forces fought on every front while the home front produced the ships, planes, and weapons that supplied the entire Allied war effort.
