Overview

Britain stood alone against Nazi Germany from June 1940 to June 1941, surviving the Battle of Britain, the Blitz, and the U-boat siege of the Atlantic. As the arsenal, airfield, and launching pad of Allied operations in Europe, Britain was the indispensable partner without which D-Day and the liberation of Western Europe could not have happened.
