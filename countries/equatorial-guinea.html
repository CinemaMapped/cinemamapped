Overview

Spanish-controlled Fernando Po (now Bioko Island, part of Equatorial Guinea) was a neutral port in the Gulf of Guinea where Axis ships sheltered. In January 1942, British Special Operations Executive operatives carried out Operation Postmaster — stealing a German and Italian vessel from the neutral harbour in a daring midnight raid.
