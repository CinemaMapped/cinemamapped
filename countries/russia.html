Eastern Front

WWII Film Locations in Russia

37 locations · 12 titles

Overview

The Soviet Union bore the greatest military and civilian losses of any nation in the war — an estimated 27 million dead. The Eastern Front, stretching from the Arctic to the Black Sea, was the largest and most brutal land campaign in history, including the Siege of Leningrad, the Battle of Stalingrad, and the destruction of Army Group Centre in Operation Bagration.

CinemaMapped has mapped 37 real WWII film locations in Russia, drawn from 12 titles. Each pin marks the actual historical place depicted in the film - not a filming location, but where the events happened.
