Overview

The Netherlands was occupied by Germany from May 1940 to May 1945 — five years that included the starvation of the Hunger Winter of 1944-45, the deportation of 75 percent of its Jewish population (the highest proportion in Western Europe), and the failed liberation attempt at Arnhem. The Dutch resistance and those who sheltered Jews are at the heart of some of WWII's most important stories.
