WWII Film Locations in Italy

Overview

Italy fought alongside Germany as an Axis power until September 1943, when it signed an armistice and was subsequently invaded from the south by Allied forces. The Italian campaign then became one of the war's most grinding fronts — the Wehrmacht fought a stubborn defensive war up the peninsula all the way to April 1945.
