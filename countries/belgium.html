Overview

Belgium was occupied by Germany from May 1940 to September 1944. The country was the scene of the Battle of the Bulge in December 1944-January 1945 — Hitler's last major offensive in the west, which inflicted some of the heaviest American casualties of the entire war, particularly around Bastogne.
