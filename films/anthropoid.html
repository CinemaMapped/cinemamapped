Anthropoid Filming Locations

2 locations · Czech Republic

About

Based on the true story of Operation Anthropoid — the Czech and Slovak paratroopers trained by the British who assassinated Reinhard Heydrich, one of the Holocaust's chief architects, in Prague in 1942. The assassination and its brutal aftermath are meticulously recreated.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Czech Republic.
