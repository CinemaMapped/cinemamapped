Nacht und Nebel (Night and Fog) — Where the Story Was Set

3 real historical locations · France, Poland

About

Alain Resnais' landmark 1956 documentary short intercutting contemporary footage of empty concentration camp grounds with archive footage of the camps in operation. One of the first films to confront the Holocaust directly, its final question — who is responsible? — remains unanswered.

CinemaMapped has mapped 3 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning France, Poland.
