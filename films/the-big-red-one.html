The Big Red One Filming Locations

2 locations · France, Tunisia

About

Sam Fuller's autobiographical film follows a squad of the 1st Infantry Division from North Africa through Sicily, Normandy, Belgium, and the liberation of a concentration camp. Based on Fuller's own wartime experiences, it captures both the camaraderie and senselessness of combat.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning France, Tunisia.
