The Guns of Navarone — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Greece

About

A 1961 adventure classic following an Allied commando team sent to destroy two massive German guns on a Greek island blocking a naval operation. A template for the behind-enemy-lines genre that spawned countless imitators.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Greece.
