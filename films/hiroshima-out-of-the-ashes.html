Hiroshima: Out of the Ashes — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Japan

About

A 1990 American TV film depicting the aftermath of the atomic bombing from multiple perspectives — Japanese civilians, American prisoners of war, and a German Jesuit priest. One of the first productions to show the bomb's human consequences in unflinching detail.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Japan.
