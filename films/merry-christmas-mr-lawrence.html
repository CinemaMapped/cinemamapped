Batavia (Jakarta), Java

The colonial capital falls to Japanese forces in March 1942. Dutch and British prisoners are processed here before transport to inland camps across Java.

Batavia was the capital of the Dutch East Indies. It was renamed Djakarta by the Japanese occupation government and later became Jakarta, the Indonesian capital.
