4 real historical locations · Egypt, Libya

About

A 1967 American war film about a mission to blow up Rommel's fuel depot at Tobruk before the El Alamein offensive — using German-Jewish soldiers from Palestine disguised as prisoners escorted by British Commandos through the Libyan desert.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Egypt, Libya.

Tobruk Harbour, Libya

The target of the Allied raid: Rommel's fuel depot at Tobruk harbour. If destroyed, the Afrika Korps advance into Egypt and toward Suez grinds to a halt.

Operation Agreement (13 September 1942) aimed to destroy Tobruk's fuel facilities. It was a catastrophic failure; most raiders were killed or captured. The film dramatises an alternative mission.
