Hacksaw Ridge — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Japan

About

Mel Gibson's film based on the true story of Desmond Doss, a conscientious objector who refused to carry a weapon yet won the Medal of Honor as a combat medic at Okinawa in 1945. He single-handedly saved 75 men by lowering them down an escarpment under fire.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Japan.
