13 Minutes — Where the Story Was Set

About

A German carpenter comes within 13 minutes of killing Hitler with a bomb planted in a Munich beer hall in 1939. Based on the true story of Georg Elser, it is a taut thriller about one ordinary man who risked everything to change history — and why it failed.
