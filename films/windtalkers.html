Windtalkers — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Northern Mariana Islands, United States

About

John Woo's film about a Marine assigned to protect a Navajo Code Talker on Saipan — with secret orders to kill him if capture is imminent. The Navajo code, based on an unwritten language, was never broken by Japan and played a crucial role in Pacific communications.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Northern Mariana Islands, United States.
