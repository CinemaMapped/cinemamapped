Captain Corelli's Mandolin — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Greece

About

Set on the Greek island of Kefalonia during Italian and German occupation, this romantic drama follows a young Greek woman's relationship with an Italian officer. Their idyll is destroyed when the Germans massacred the Italian garrison after Italy's 1943 armistice.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Greece.

Sami harbor, Cephalonia

The harbor where Italian troops first arrive on the island and where the German warships later bring the force that will overwhelm them.
