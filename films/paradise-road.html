Paradise Road — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Indonesia, Singapore

About

Based on the true experiences of Allied women prisoners held in Japanese camps in Sumatra, who formed a vocal orchestra — singing transcriptions of classical pieces — as an act of defiance and spiritual sustenance. Glenn Close leads the story of women's courage under captivity.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Indonesia, Singapore.

In February 1942 Japanese forces executed 22 Australian Army nurses on Bangka Island beach after the Vyner Brooke was sunk. Only one nurse, Vivian Bullwinkel, survived to testify at war crimes trials.

The Palembang camps held Dutch, British, and Australian women POWs in Sumatra until 1945. The vocal orchestra was real: conductor Margaret Dryburgh and Norah Chambers organised it from 1943.

Singapore (SS Vyner Brooke departure)

The Vyner Brooke was bombed on 14 February 1942. Of the 200+ passengers, 53 Australian nurses were aboard. 12 died in the sinking; 22 were executed on the beach; 24 survived three years of captivity.
