The Battle of Britain — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · France, United Kingdom

About

A 1969 British epic depicting the RAF's defence against the Luftwaffe during the summer of 1940 — the aerial campaign that forced Hitler to postpone his invasion. Featuring actual Spitfires and Hurricanes, it remains the most accurate recreation of air combat in cinema history.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning France, United Kingdom.
