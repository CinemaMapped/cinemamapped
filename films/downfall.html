Downfall — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Germany

About

Bruno Ganz delivers a legendary performance as Adolf Hitler in his final days in the Berlin bunker in April 1945. Based on the memoirs of Hitler's secretary, the film humanises the monster to show how ordinary people enabled and served him until the very end — a landmark of German cinema.
