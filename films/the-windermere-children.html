The Windermere Children — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Czech Republic, United Kingdom

About

A BBC film depicting the true story of 300 young Holocaust survivors brought to the Lake District in 1945 to begin recovery. One of the few WWII films focused entirely on healing rather than fighting, it shows the enormous task of rebuilding lives shattered by the camps.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Czech Republic, United Kingdom.

Theresienstadt, Czech Republic

Many of the Windermere children passed through Theresienstadt, the Nazi 'model' ghetto used to deceive the Red Cross, before deportation to Auschwitz-Birkenau.

Theresienstadt held over 144,000 Jews. More than 33,000 died there from disease and starvation. A further 88,000 were deported to extermination camps. It was liberated by the Red Army on 8 May 1945.
