The Windermere Children — Where the Story Was Set

1 real historical location · United Kingdom

About

A BBC film depicting the true story of 300 young Holocaust survivors brought to the Lake District in 1945 to begin recovery. One of the few WWII films focused entirely on healing rather than fighting, it shows the enormous task of rebuilding lives shattered by the camps.
