Red Tails — Where the Story Was Set

2 real historical locations · Italy, Romania

About

George Lucas' film about the Tuskegee Airmen — the African-American pilots of the 332nd Fighter Group who proved their worth over Italy and became legendary for never losing a bomber under their escort. A story of excellence defeating prejudice in the skies over Europe.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Italy, Romania.
