The Resistance Banker — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Netherlands

About

A Dutch film based on the true story of Walraven van Hall, a bank director who secretly embezzled millions from his own bank to fund the Dutch resistance — paying for false documents, hiding Jews, supporting strikes — until his arrest and execution in March 1945.
