The Auschwitz Report Filming Locations

2 locations · Poland, Slovakia

About

A Czech-Slovak film based on the true story of two Slovakian prisoners who escaped Auschwitz-Birkenau in 1944 and compiled a detailed report documenting the mass murder — the Vrba-Wetzler Report, eventually delivered to the Allies and the Vatican.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Poland, Slovakia.
