The Great Escape — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Germany, Poland

About

John Sturges' classic 1963 adventure based on the true mass escape from Stalag Luft III in March 1944, in which 76 Allied prisoners broke out through hand-dug tunnels. Only three made it to freedom; 50 were executed on Hitler's personal orders. Steve McQueen leads the all-star cast.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Germany, Poland.
