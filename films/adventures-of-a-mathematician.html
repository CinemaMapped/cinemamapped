Adventures of a Mathematician — Where the Story Was Set

3 real historical locations · Ukraine, United States

About

The story of Stanislaw Ulam, a Polish-Jewish mathematician who fled Europe for America and joined the Manhattan Project, helping design the hydrogen bomb. It explores the moral weight of scientific discovery and the immigrant experience in wartime America.

CinemaMapped has mapped 3 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Ukraine, United States.
