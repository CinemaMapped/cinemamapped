The Shadow in My Eye — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Denmark, United Kingdom

About

A Danish film depicting the Copenhagen School bombing of March 1945, when the Royal Air Force accidentally bombed a French school near a Gestapo headquarters, killing 86 Danish children and 18 adults. A rare examination of Allied mistakes and civilian casualties.
