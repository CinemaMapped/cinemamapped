Sophie Scholl: The Final Days Filming Locations

2 locations · Germany

About

Based on Gestapo interrogation transcripts, this German film depicts the last six days of Sophie Scholl — the 21-year-old who distributed anti-Nazi leaflets with the White Rose resistance group and was arrested, tried, and guillotined in February 1943.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Germany.
