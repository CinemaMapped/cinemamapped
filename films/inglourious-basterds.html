About

Quentin Tarantino's alternate-history thriller set in occupied France, following a group of Jewish-American soldiers hunting Nazis and a Jewish cinema owner plotting her own revenge. Deliberately revisionist, it asks what cinema might do if it could rewrite history — and delivers a spectacularly violent answer.
