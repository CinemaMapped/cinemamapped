A Small Light — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Netherlands

About

A biographical drama about Miep Gies, the Dutch office worker who helped hide Anne Frank and her family in the Secret Annex in Amsterdam for over two years. It humanises those who risked their lives daily to protect the people they sheltered.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Netherlands.
