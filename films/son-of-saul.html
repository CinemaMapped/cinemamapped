Son of Saul — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Poland

About

Laszlo Nemes' Hungarian film, shot in extreme close-up, follows Saul Auslaender, a Sonderkommando at Auschwitz-Birkenau forced to assist in the genocide he is part of. When he finds a body he believes is his son's, he embarks on a desperate mission to give the boy a proper burial.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Poland.
