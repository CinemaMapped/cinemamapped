Stalingrad (1993) — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Russia

About

A German film following Wehrmacht soldiers through the catastrophic Stalingrad campaign of 1942-43 — from their arrival in the city to the destruction of the Sixth Army. One of the most honest German films about the Eastern Front, told entirely from the losers' perspective.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Russia.
