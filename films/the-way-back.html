The Way Back — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · India, Mongolia, Russia

About

Peter Weir's survival film based on the disputed memoir of Slawomir Rawicz, depicting the escape of Polish prisoners from a Siberian gulag in 1941 and their 4,000-mile trek through Mongolia, the Gobi Desert, and the Himalayas to British India.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning India, Mongolia, Russia.
