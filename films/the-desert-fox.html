The Desert Fox — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Egypt, France, Germany, Libya

About

A 1951 film portraying Field Marshal Erwin Rommel as a brilliant soldier of honour who turned against Hitler after Normandy and was implicated in the July 1944 plot. James Mason's Rommel became the template for the contested myth of the honourable German officer.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Egypt, France, Germany, Libya.
