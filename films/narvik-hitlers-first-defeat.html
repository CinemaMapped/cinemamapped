Narvik: Hitler's First Defeat — Where the Story Was Set

1 real historical location · Norway

About

A Norwegian film depicting the Battle of Narvik in April-May 1940, where Norwegian and Allied forces briefly recaptured the port from German forces — Hitler's first military setback. Told through the story of a Norwegian woman and her German-soldier husband on opposite sides.
