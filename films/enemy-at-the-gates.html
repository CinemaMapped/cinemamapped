About

Jean-Jacques Annaud's thriller fictionalises the Battle of Stalingrad through a duel between Soviet sniper Vasily Zaitsev and a German expert marksman. While debated historically, it vividly captures the apocalyptic urban warfare that consumed the city across the winter of 1942-43.
