About

A landmark 1978 American miniseries following two families — one Jewish, one a rising SS officer — across the entire Nazi period from 1935 to 1945. Enormously influential at the time, it introduced millions of viewers to the Holocaust and prompted West Germany's first national broadcast on the subject.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Poland.
