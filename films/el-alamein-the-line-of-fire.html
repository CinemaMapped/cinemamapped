El Alamein: The Line of Fire — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Egypt, Libya

About

An Italian war film following young soldiers through the North African campaign and the Second Battle of El Alamein in 1942. Shot on location in the Egyptian desert, it portrays the forgotten experience of Italian soldiers caught in Rommel's ultimately doomed desert war.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Egypt, Libya.
