About

A 1965 British war film based on Operation Gunnerside — the Norwegian resistance mission to destroy the heavy water plant at Vemork in 1943, denying the Germans a key component for a potential atomic weapon. Kirk Douglas leads the saboteurs through the Norwegian winter.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Norway.
