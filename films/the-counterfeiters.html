The Counterfeiters — Where the Story Was Set

2 real historical locations · Austria, Germany

About

Stefan Ruzowitzky's Oscar-winning Austrian film based on Operation Bernhard — the Nazi scheme to counterfeit British and American currency using Jewish prisoners at Sachsenhausen. The moral dilemma: participate and survive, or sabotage the operation and likely die.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Austria, Germany.

Locations (2)
Sachsenhausen concentration camp, Oranienburg

Block 19 of Sachsenhausen — where 142 Jewish prisoners run Operation Bernhard, the largest counterfeiting operation in history.
