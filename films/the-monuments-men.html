About

The Monuments Men is a WWII film set on the Western Front during 1944-1945. CinemaMapped has mapped 3 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Austria, France, Germany. Each pin marks the actual place depicted - not where the film was shot, but where the historical events happened.
