The Railway Man — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Singapore, Thailand

About

The true story of Eric Lomax, a British POW tortured by the Japanese on the Burma-Thailand Railway who spent forty years consumed by trauma and hatred — until an unexpected reunion with his torturer led to forgiveness. Colin Firth and Jeremy Irvine play Lomax at different ages.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Singapore, Thailand.

Hellfire Pass (Konyu Cutting), Thailand

The nightmarish cutting through solid rock where Eric Lomax and fellow POWs are forced to work around the clock by torchlight. The worst stretch of the Death...

Konyu Cutting took 3,000 POWs and Asian labourers to complete in 12 weeks during 1943. They worked 18-hour shifts with hammers and chisels. Hundreds died from exhaustion, cholera, and beatings.

Kanchanaburi, Thailand

The town at the southern terminus of the Burma-Thailand Railway where the Bridge on the River Kwai stands, and where Eric Lomax returns decades later to conf...

Kanchanaburi was the base camp for the Death Railway. The Allied war cemetery there holds 6,982 graves. The Bridge on the River Kwai still carries a tourist railway.

Changi Prison, Singapore

Changi held up to 87,000 Allied POWs after the fall of Singapore. It became a symbol of resilience. The site is now Singapore's international airport precinct.
