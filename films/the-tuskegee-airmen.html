About

A 1995 HBO film about the first African-American military aviators — the men who trained at Tuskegee, Alabama and became the 332nd Fighter Group. Starring Laurence Fishburne, it brought the story of these pioneering pilots to mainstream audiences before Red Tails.

CinemaMapped has mapped 2 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Italy, United States.
