About

A 2001 TV film depicting the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising of April-May 1943, in which Jewish fighters armed with improvised weapons held off the SS for four weeks — the longest urban resistance of WWII. Hank Azaria, David Schwimmer, and Jon Voight lead the cast.
