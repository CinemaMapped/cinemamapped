Talvisota — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Finland

About

The definitive Finnish film about the Winter War — the Soviet invasion of Finland in 1939-40 — following Finnish reservists defending their homeland against overwhelming Soviet forces. Finland's extraordinary resistance, killing four Soviet soldiers for every Finn lost, is chronicled with grim realism.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Finland.
