Grave of the Fireflies — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Japan

About

Isao Takahata's Studio Ghibli masterpiece follows two orphaned Japanese siblings struggling to survive after their mother dies in a firebombing raid near the war's end. One of the most devastating anti-war films ever made — all the more powerful for its animated form.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Japan.
