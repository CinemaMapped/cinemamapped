Tora! Tora! Tora! — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · United States

About

A joint American-Japanese production depicting the attack on Pearl Harbor from both perspectives simultaneously. Released in 1970, it remains the most historically accurate account of the attack — showing the meticulous Japanese planning and the American intelligence failures that allowed it to succeed.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning United States.
