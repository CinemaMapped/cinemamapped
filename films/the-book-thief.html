The Book Thief — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Germany

About

Based on Markus Zusak's novel narrated by Death, this film follows a young German girl living near Munich who steals books and hides a Jewish man in the basement during the war. A story about the power of words in a world trying to destroy them.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Germany.
