The Tattooist of Auschwitz — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Poland, Slovakia

About

Based on Heather Morris's bestselling novel about Lale Sokolov, a Slovakian Jewish prisoner who tattooed numbers onto his fellow inmates at Auschwitz and fell in love with one of them. A love story that sustained two people through three years of the Holocaust.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Poland, Slovakia.
