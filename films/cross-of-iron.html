Cross of Iron — Where the Story Was Set

4 real historical locations · Russia

About

Sam Peckinpah's brutal 1977 film follows a German sergeant on the Eastern Front who despises his officers' ideology more than he does the enemy. One of the few major WWII films told entirely from the German soldier's perspective, capturing the nihilistic horror of the Eastern Front.

CinemaMapped has mapped 4 real historical locations where the story takes place, spanning Russia.

The key Black Sea port contested by Soviet amphibious landings and the German mountain troops holding the Kuban bridgehead.
