RAF Bassingbourn, Cambridgeshire

Memphis Belle — United Kingdom

Scene

The final mission of B-17 bomber 'Memphis Belle' — the crew's 25th and last bombing run over Nazi Germany.

History

The Memphis Belle and her crew became the first USAAF heavy bomber crew to complete 25 missions in the European Theatre. Their survival rate was exceptional — at the time, the average crew was expected to last 15 missions before being killed or captured.
