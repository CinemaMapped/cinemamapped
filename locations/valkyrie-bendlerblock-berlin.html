Bendlerblock, Berlin

Valkyrie — Germany

Scene

German Army HQ in Berlin — where Operation Valkyrie is launched and where the conspirators are arrested and executed.

History

When news of Hitler's survival reached Berlin, the coup collapsed. Stauffenberg and three fellow conspirators were shot in the Bendlerblock courtyard at midnight on July 20. Over 200 people connected to the plot were eventually executed, many by slow hanging with piano wire.
