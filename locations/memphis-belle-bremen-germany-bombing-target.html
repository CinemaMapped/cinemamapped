Bremen, Germany (bombing target)

Memphis Belle — Germany

Scene

The Memphis Belle's final bombing mission targets the U-boat shipyards at Bremen.

History

Bremen's submarine yards were a priority USAAF target throughout 1943. Bombing U-boat facilities was considered essential to winning the Battle of the Atlantic, though the hardened pens themselves were nearly impossible to destroy from the air.
