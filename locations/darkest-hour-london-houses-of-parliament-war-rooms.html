London — Houses of Parliament & War Rooms

Darkest Hour — United Kingdom

Scene

May 1940 — Churchill's first weeks as Prime Minister. The Cabinet War Rooms, Parliament debates, and the decision to fight on.

History

Churchill became Prime Minister on May 10, 1940 — the same day Germany launched its western offensive. His War Cabinet debated negotiating with Hitler on May 26-28. Churchill's decision to fight on changed the course of the war.
