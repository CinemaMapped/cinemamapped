Central Germany — Harz region

Fury — Germany

Scene

April 1945 — Sherman tank crew 'Wardaddy' fights deep into Nazi Germany. Final stand at a crossroads against SS troops.

History

By April 1945, Allied tank crews were pushing deep into Germany against fanatical SS resistance and Volkssturm militia. The Harz mountains were one of the last pockets of organised German resistance before the final collapse.
