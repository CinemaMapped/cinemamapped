Zhejiang province, China

Thirty Seconds Over Tokyo — China

Scene

The Raiders crash-land or bail out over Japanese-occupied China — sheltered by Chinese civilians and guerrillas before escaping.

History

Japan's reprisal for Chinese civilian assistance to the Doolittle Raiders was catastrophic. Operation Sei-go deployed 100,000 Japanese troops through Zhejiang and Jiangxi provinces, killing an estimated 250,000 Chinese civilians. Every village suspected of helping American airmen was destroyed.
