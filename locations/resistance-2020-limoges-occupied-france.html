Limoges / Occupied France

Resistance (2020) — France

Scene

Marcel Marceau helps rescue Jewish orphans and smuggle them across the Alps to Switzerland. Based on a true story.

History

Marcel Marceau, later world-famous as a mime artist, was a member of the French Jewish Scouts and helped rescue hundreds of Jewish children during the occupation. He guided groups across the Alps to neutral Switzerland, using his mime skills to keep children quiet during dangerous crossings.
