Rhodes / Aegean island

The Guns of Navarone — Greece

Scene

Allied commando team destroys massive German guns on a fictional Aegean island, allowing the evacuation of 2,000 soldiers.

History

The Dodecanese islands were a genuine strategic prize — German control of Aegean gun batteries blocked Allied shipping and supply routes to the Eastern Mediterranean. The failed British attempt to seize the Dodecanese in 1943 (Operation Accolade) is the real-world inspiration.
