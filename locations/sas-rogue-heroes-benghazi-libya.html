Benghazi, Libya

SAS: Rogue Heroes — Libya

Scene

The SAS raids Benghazi harbour and Axis airfields — destroying supplies and aircraft in coordinated attacks deep behind enemy lines with jeep-mounted machine guns.

History

The SAS conducted multiple raids around Benghazi between 1941 and 1943, sinking ships in the harbour and destroying aircraft on the ground. These operations significantly disrupted Rommel's supply lines. The Long Range Desert Group provided navigation support for many of the raids across hundreds of miles of open desert.
