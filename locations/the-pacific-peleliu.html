Peleliu

The Pacific — Palau

Scene

Episodes 5–7 — Battle of Peleliu. One of the bloodiest and most costly battles in the Pacific. Portrayed with brutal realism.

History

The Battle of Peleliu (September–November 1944) was predicted to last four days. It lasted over two months. Of the 10,900 Marines who landed, 6,526 were killed or wounded. Many historians question whether the island needed to be taken at all.
