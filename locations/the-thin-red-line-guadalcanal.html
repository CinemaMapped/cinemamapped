Guadalcanal

The Thin Red Line — Solomon Islands

Scene

Philosophical meditation on the Battle of Guadalcanal — American soldiers assault Japanese-held Hill 210.

History

The fighting on Guadalcanal was distinguished by its ferocity and the conditions — equatorial jungle, malaria, and supply shortages that killed almost as many men as combat. Both sides fought to exhaustion before Japan finally evacuated in February 1943.
