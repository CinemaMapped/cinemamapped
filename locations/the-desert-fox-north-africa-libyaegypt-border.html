North Africa — Libya/Egypt border

The Desert Fox — Libya

Scene

Rommel's Afrika Korps campaigns across North Africa — the strategic genius and ultimate defeat of the Desert Fox.

History

Rommel's Afrika Korps advanced 1,400 km into Egypt at its peak, threatening the Suez Canal. His defeat at El Alamein in November 1942 — and the subsequent Allied landings in Morocco — ended Axis hopes in North Africa. Rommel was later forced to commit suicide for his role in the July 20 plot.
