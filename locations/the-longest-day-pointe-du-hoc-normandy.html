Pointe du Hoc, Normandy

The Longest Day — France

Scene

US Army Rangers scale the 30-metre cliffs at Pointe du Hoc to destroy German artillery.

History

The 2nd Ranger Battalion scaled Pointe du Hoc under fire on D-Day, only to find the guns had been moved inland. They found and destroyed them anyway, then held the position for two days against German counterattacks with only 90 of 225 men still combat-effective.
