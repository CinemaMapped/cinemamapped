Stadelheim Prison, Munich

Sophie Scholl: The Final Days — Germany

Scene

February 22 1943 — Sophie Scholl is executed by guillotine at Stadelheim Prison, just four days after her arrest.

History

Sophie, Hans, and Christoph Probst were tried before the People's Court under Judge Roland Freisler, who screamed at them and denied them the right to speak. Sentenced at noon, they were executed by guillotine at 5pm the same day. Sophie was 21. Prison guards later reported she showed no fear.
